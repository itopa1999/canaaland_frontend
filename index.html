Welcome Address

Dearly beloved youths, esteemed guests, and fellow servants of the Most High God, It is with immense joy, unbridled expectation, and a deep sense of reverence that I welcome you to the YPE 2025 Explosion, themed “The Supernatural” here at CAC Canaan Land DCC Headquarters. As we gather from April 16th to 19th, 2025, we are self-assured of an extraordinary encounter, one that will unleash the mighty move of God in our lives and propel us into the unfathomable mysteries of the Supernatural.

In this sacred moment, we stand at the confluence of the natural and the Supernatural, where the boundaries of human understanding are stretched, and the limitless possibilities of God’s power are unveiled. As we embark on this journey, we are reminded of the Apostle Paul’s profound prayer in Ephesians 1:17-21, where he implores the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of Glory, to grant us the spirit of wisdom and of revelation in the knowledge of Him.

Over the next few days, we will be navigating the vast expanse of God’s Supernatural Power, exploring the depths of His wisdom, and discovering the hidden riches of His grace. We have an incredible lineup of anointed speakers, worship leaders, and ministry teams who will be guiding us on this journey—imparting wisdom and empowering us to walk in the Supernatural.

To our esteemed guests, Youths from different districts, students from school fellowships, and everyone who has joined us for this momentous occasion, we extend a warm and sincere welcome! We are honored to have you with us, and we pray that you will experience a Supernatural encounter with God that will forever alter the path of your life.

As we gather in this sacred space, let us approach this event with expectation, humility, and a willingness to surrender to the Supernatural power of God. May we leave this place with a renewed sense of purpose, passion, and power to fulfill the Great Commission. May our lives be forever transformed by the Supernatural presence of God.

Before we begin this extraordinary journey, let us take a moment to acknowledge the sovereign presence of God among us. Let us breathe in the atmosphere of expectation, and let us declare our readiness to receive all that God has in store for us.

Thank you, and may God richly bless you.
Amen!

DCC Youth Coordinator
FATIMILEHIN EMMANUEL ISEOLUWA
